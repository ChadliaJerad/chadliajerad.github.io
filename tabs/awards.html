Selected Publications

Adaptive Learning in Neural Networks: A Meta-Learning Approach
J. Smith, A. Johnson, M. Lee | ICML 2025

We present a novel meta-learning framework that enables neural networks to adapt to new tasks with minimal fine-tuning, achieving state-of-the-art results across multiple benchmarks.

Efficient Transformers for Low-Resource Languages
J. Smith, K. Chen | ACL 2024

This work introduces a transformer architecture optimized for languages with limited training data, demonstrating significant improvements in translation quality.

Interpretable Deep Learning for Medical Diagnosis
J. Smith, R. Patel, S. Williams | Nature Machine Intelligence 2024

We develop an interpretable deep learning system for medical image analysis that provides clinically meaningful explanations alongside predictions.
