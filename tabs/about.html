About Me

I am an Associate Professor at the National School of Computer Science (ENSI), University of Manouba, Tunisia, where I teach and conduct research in Embedded Systems and Cyber-Physical Systems. I am passionate about building predictable, real-time, and formally verifiable systems — from bare-metal embedded platforms to distributed cyber-physical infrastructures. I have held research positions at UC Berkeley as a Fulbright Visiting Scholar (2016–2017) and as a Visiting Scholar (2022–2023), collaborating with Prof. Edward A. Lee on the Lingua Franca and Accessors projects.

Short Bio

Dr. Chadlia JERAD is an Associate Professor at ENSI, University of Manouba, Tunisia, specializing in Embedded Systems, Cyber-Physical Systems, and Real-Time Systems. She holds a Doctorate in Electrical Engineering from ENIT (2008) and has been a Fulbright Visiting Scholar and a Visiting Scholar at UC Berkeley's EECS Department, collaborating with Prof. Edward A. Lee. She leads and co-leads several international research initiatives funded by DAAD, serves on program committees of top venues (RTAS, RTSS, DATE), and has co-authored publications in IEEE ESL, ACM TECS, Proceedings of the IEEE, and IEEE Access.
