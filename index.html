About Me

I am an Associate Professor at the National School of Computer Science (ENSI), University of Manouba, TN, where I teach and conduct research in Embedded Systems and Cyber-Physical Systems. I am passionate about building predictable, real-time, and formally verifiable systems — from bare-metal embedded platforms to distributed cyber-physical infrastructures. I have held research positions at UC Berkeley as a Fulbright Visiting Scholar (2016–2017) and as a Visiting Scholar (2022–2023), collaborating with Prof. Edward A. Lee on the Lingua Franca and Accessors projects.

Software

  • Lingua Franca — A polyglot coordination language for concurrent and distributed systems, enabling deterministic execution across heterogeneous platforms.
  • Accessors — A framework for building and analyzing access control systems, with a focus on formal verification and security policy enforcement.
